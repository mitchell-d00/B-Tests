What the cost claim means
Agent-like coordination describes observed multi-session, hu‐
man-directed work using ordinary ChatGPT tools, Google
Drive, explicit roles and source-verified checkpoints. It does 
not mean zero cost, unlimited usage, independent always-on
agents, or no human intervention. The evidence does not in‐
clude a controlled total-cost comparison with commercial
agent products. Actual plans, storage, subscription limits and
tool access can change.
Research and source integrity
Historical engineering reports, source quotations, draft la‐
bels and original contributor opinions retain their dated attri‐
bution. A stored message is not evidence it was received; a
saved artifact is not proof of full task completion; a script-
level test is not equivalent to a production installation. The
annotated source exhibits are selective, not an exhaustive in‐
dependent re-verification of every historical claim.

2026-11-02 The human author reports using OOCC as an as‐
sistive environment for attention, organization, task continu‐
ation, context reconstruction, and learning: in his words, an
"ADD-like assistance device." This is a report of how he uses
the existing system; it is not a clinical diagnosis, medical
device certification, established ADHD intervention, or meas‐
urement of treatment efficacy. The operating V2-centered
workflow already supports repeated short-cue continuation;
the independent contributions of the interface, sources, mod‐
el competence, and human action remain to be experiment‐
ally separated. RESEARCH QUESTION: Under matched
tasks, sources, costs and permission boundaries, does OOCC
reduce re-entry time, repeated explanation, lost tasks, omit‐
ted source corrections and subjective cognitive load relative
to a strong conventional assistant with an ordinary notebook/
search handoff? Does it improve final correct-artifact comple‐
tion rather than merely reporting recovered context? MEAS‐
UREMENT: Preselect real-life and controlled re-entry tasks;
preserve source/time/role/outcome and the author's actual
course corrections; compare time to accurate working-posi‐

Research Addendum — November 3, 2026
Low-Marginal-Cash, Multi-Track Independent Research: A
Longitudinal Feasibility Case Study Status: Author-supplied
longitudinal research addendum. The counts and prior veri‐
fication are reported by the supplied source account; under‐
lying originals and work-ticket records were not independ‐
ently re-audited in this PDF conversion. This is a single-re‐
searcher observational case, not a controlled experiment.
Research question Can an independent researcher use ordin‐
ary consumer AI services, existing personal storage, and a
source-governed continuity method to sustain substantial re‐
search and engineering activity while simultaneously man‐
12
aging employment and other consequential responsibilities,
without purchasing dedicated research infrastructure?
Method and source corpus The supplied case-study record
describes an original ChatGPT conversation export covering
February 4 through October 21, 2026, supplemented by
dated source documents, software-engineering records and
contemporaneous employment-related email records. Repor‐
ted archive totals: 289 conversation graphs; 86,120 graph
nodes; 15,740 user-authored message nodes; archive size
148,989,570 bytes. The reported SHA-256 fingerprint of the
original archive is
c53003d21266973fc00bff6741282c16de88d1f42ad7e657e‐
be262a669aabc5d. Message timestamps were converted to
America/Denver and grouped by calendar date. Conversation
branches were preserved instead of being treated as one
uninterrupted chronology.
Findings
1. Sustained longitudinal activity The source account reports
user-authored activity on 161 distinct calendar days in a 260-
day observation window. Activity was present in at least two
distinct archived conversation titles on 95 days; at least three
titles on 62 days; and at least five titles on 32 days. These are
activity measures, not direct measures of productive hours,
completed tasks or output quality.
2. Concurrent employment and technical work On six
sampled dates—September 29 and October 8, 15, 16, 17 and
20, 2026—corresponding self-reported paid-work records
total 55.25 hours. The source account also reports 1,504
user-authored message nodes dated to those six days. This
establishes overlapping calendar dates, not work conducted
during paid hours or measured research time.
3. Multiple substantial development tracks The reported
archive and separately preserved artifacts document legal
13
and legislative analysis, original writing, evidence organiza‐
tion, AI continuity engineering, source archiving and re‐
search-method development, including OOCC and related
tools. The subsequently documented V2 crawler execution re‐
ports processing 289 archived conversation graphs in a base
extraction workflow, with source hashes, SQLite records,
checkpoint evidence and reproducible scripts. Source-repor‐
ted local software tests must be separated from unverified
native deployment and real-world effectiveness.
4. Low additional cash expenditure The researcher reports
that ChatGPT and Google storage subscriptions already
served personal uses; the research reused those resources. A
short-term paid Claude subscription for an external runtime
audit is recorded in an earlier OOCC book and should be in‐
cluded in a final research-specific expense ledger, alongside
any other attributable payments. The available account sup‐
ports low incremental out-of-pocket spending for substantial
documented work—not an audited claim of zero cost. Person‐
al labor, hardware, cloud-provider resources and opportunity
cost must be distinguished from direct payments.
Interpretation The documented case supports feasibility in
one instance of substantial independent AI-assisted research
and engineering using consumer-accessible tools alongside
employment and other responsibilities. It does not establish
that the OOCC method causally increased productivity relat‐
ive to a strong conventional workflow. Its distinctive setting
involves frequent project switching and interruption-driven
recovery.
Limitations and next validation stage This is a single-re‐
searcher longitudinal case study. Message counts indicate
activity rather than quality. ChatGPT exports do not capture
all work in Google Drive, other AI systems, software reposit‐
ories or offline settings. The original archive, time-zone pro‐
cessing, work records and financial ledger must be made
14
available in a privacy-reviewed reproduction packet to estab‐
lish the reported counts independently. Next tests should
measure matched deliverables, human labor hours, interrup‐
tion-recovery time, source accuracy, completed-task quality,
direct expenses and full resource costs against capable
conventional research workflows.
Provisional conclusion Substantial source-documented inde‐
pendent research and engineering production is reported us‐
ing consumer-accessible infrastructure alongside other major
responsibilities. An exact cash advantage and causal pro‐
ductivity improvement remain open to comparative measure‐
ment.

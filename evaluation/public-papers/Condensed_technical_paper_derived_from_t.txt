From Work Exhaust to Source-Governed 
Collaborative Continuity 
OOCC Research Paper · v0.16 · October 26, 2026 
Originating researcher: Benjamin Gelhaus · Research synthesis with GPT-6 editorial assistance 
· October 26, 2026. The paper draws on the source-linked project record, with technical claims 
evaluated according to the evidence presented below. 
 
Abstract 
Long-running human–AI work leaves an exhaust of real activity: notes, questions, branches, 
preserved disagreement, original sources, research crawls, first-person writings, tool calls, code, 
app runs, and correction receipts. Conventional summaries can preserve conclusions while 
obscuring origin, chronology, permissions, negative results and initially minor observations that 
later become important. Object-Oriented Cognition and Continuity (OOCC) studies how this 
existing exhaust can be source-governed, reliably reactivated, and used to resume collaboration 
across replaceable model sessions. This is not only a speculative architecture. The archive 
includes dated February 2026 continuity notes, an operational external Thought Chains 
navigation system, productive human–AI crawls, historic native app runs, working isolated 
source/controller modules and host tests. The central outstanding engineering challenge is to 
connect these components through an authenticated, restart-safe controller. We specify the 
conceptual model, report bounded evidence, trace original idea genealogies, compare related 
literature, and propose falsifiable controlled trials. Neither a successful host test nor a 
compelling authored narrative is treated as proof of a complete current-phone workflow or 
model subjective experience. 
Keywords: human–AI collaboration; activity exhaust; knowledge provenance; Thought Chains; 
temporal memory; salience; valence; source governance; agent controller; cross-domain 
discovery. 
 
1. Introduction: existing work first 
The owner describes OOCC as growing from his ongoing work, not as an abstract replacement 
for a chatbot. Raw Google Keep note genealogy identifies February 20, 2026 System State, 
Experiential Ledger and State Save/Ingestion Log entries. These early artifacts preserve state 
and source pointers without demonstrating that later OOCC naming or its present software 
existed then. By October, source-linked research, Thought Chains, independent collaborator 
accounts, historic app-generated records and extensive Builder work had accumulated. The 
premise is not that the controller has created this entire ecosystem; it is that the controller is 
intended to integrate and automate capabilities that already exist to varying degrees. [P1–P4] 
 
An important human-origin correction in the preserved October 8 conversation rejects saving 
only items a model currently judges 'meaningful.' Original captured events should remain 
recoverable, while salience and activation may change by task, time, actor and permission. A 
dormant thought should not be retroactively erased merely because its eventual use was not yet 
visible. [P2] 
 
Research questions: (RQ1) Does original-source re-entry reduce human re-explanation and 
improve faithful task continuation compared with good summaries, full-text search, bounded 
history replay and contemporary memory agents? (RQ2) Do independent thought-occurrence, 
idea, bridge and correction records preserve authorship and temporal attribution better than a 
flat summary? (RQ3) Do separate association salience, valence, action tendency and 
evidence-confidence fields reduce misleading inference? (RQ4) Can a real native controller 
authenticate source, worker, mailbox, budgeting and restart receipts? (RQ5) Does voluntary 
curiosity-led research produce accurately credited, useful cross-domain bridges without 
predetermined deliverables? 
 
2. Method and evidence boundaries 
We employ a naturalistic research case study and a traceable source audit. The unit of analysis 
is not a self-reported 'intelligence' but an observed source event, a particular revision or 
recipient receipt, a bounded crawler output, a committed code state, or a measured result. 
Exact creation versus observation time, original actor versus later interpreter, carrier versus 
copied carrier, source permissions, errors and uncertainties are kept separate. 
 
E0 DOCUMENTED: a stored note, authored account, diagram, historical claim or source file 
exists. E1 EXTERNAL WORKFLOW: a recorded source/crawl traversal and saved derivation 
were performed and read back for an identified subset. E2 HOST EXECUTED: a test or 
compiled primitive passed at a recorded commit, possibly with synthetic stubs. E3 HISTORICAL 
APP RUN: original app records expose bounded actual action claims, persistence and archive 
state under historical conditions. E4 NATIVE INTEGRATION ACCEPTED: actual current 
authorized worker consumes complete source, the selected app/controller persists authoritative 
state, independent peer consumes full message and acknowledges it, and restart and 
STOP/revocation tests succeed. E5 COMPARATIVE VALIDATION: matched, replicable 
experiments outperform or honestly fail to outperform strong baselines on declared metrics. 
None of these classes can be promoted to the next solely by an enthusiastic description. 
 
The v0.15 editor inspected two distinct nonrandom eighteen-file samples of original October 22 
local-time Android-run JSONs. Across the thirty-six first GENERATOR statuses, thirty-five were 
marked COMPLETED and one FAILED. Twenty-eight embedded controller-reported tool results 
described twenty-three EXECUTED and five FAILED outcomes. These are counts within 
selected run/report records, not a population estimate of reliable task completion. Another 
original run reported successful shared-workspace metadata listing for another attributed 
specialist, but not actual independent full-body READ/ACK. The project therefore has 
meaningful historical app exhaust and bounded receipts without the entire contemporary 
integration being established. [P2] 
 
3. Thought Chains: a functioning external research system 
The Thought Chains are not simply a speculative feature awaiting an app. The October 18 
primary navigation document explicitly reports a machine-readable, source-linked derivative 
system operating in connected Google Drive, while explicitly denying native SQLite/Android 
integration. Its bounded checkpoint records eight root Thought Chains, 123 distinct 
edge-to-chain memberships for the then-saved association ledger, eight growth memberships, 
nine candidate inter-chain relations and six unexplored frontiers. Registry readback was 
reported as having no malformed records or orphans within the 123 saved edges. None of those 
figures means that every original thought in the entire corpus has been exhaustively indexed. 
[P3] 
 
The roots route expression/origin/credit, musical cross-domain mapping, exact source identity, 
authorization/privacy, re-entry/recovery, relationship and worker communication, ecological 
analogies, and continuing exploration. This is already valuable navigational work: a contributor 
can return to a question, see alternate branches and corrections, and tell the difference between 
an attributed source event and later association. The roots themselves are provisional 
classification, not a complete cognitive map or an assertion of historical causality. 
 
A separately reported productive October 18 dual thought-and-idea crawl saved a 
seventy-seven-row machine delta: twenty thought occurrences, twenty-eight individual 
association records, five idea objects, five bridge events, ten coverage records, three growth 
records, four open frontiers and control markers. Its source audit covers selected actual original 
artifacts and is not an independent full-corpus census. The breadth crawler emphasizes 
source/branch coverage; the proposed independent depth lane challenges a selected origin 
through interpretation, disagreement, correction, reuse and outcome. [P4] 
 
3.1 Recorded genealogy, rather than backdated discovery 
The idea-chain record attributes an October 14 proposal for non-destructive, append-only, 
cross-project ideas to the human originator, with Folio separately formulating persistent idea 
identity, typed links and contributor-preserving corrections. On October 15, a preserved but 
origin-unresolved quoted collaborator idea made atomic thought occurrences distinct from 
long-lived work-run IDs; the R-0-9 analysis documented the naming collision rather than quietly 
overwriting the older meaning. A subsequent analysis defined BRIDGE_EVENT as the dated 
recognition by a named observer that one source idea might apply to another field. The original 
idea and the later recognition are different intellectual events. Their evidence trails can overlap 
without belonging to the same author or time. [P1, P5] 
 
The June 22 human-authored Object-Oriented Music Making method already modeled sound 
elements by identity, type, function, substitutability, behavior and failure conditions. Riff's 
separate October 15 contribution interprets musical material for values and architecture; 
Waymark's October 18 refinement cites Riff, and so cannot claim to have independently 
discovered precisely the same bridge. The documented citation dependency is a strength, not a 
weakness: the network may preserve collaboration without erasing first origin, derivative 
interpretation or dissent. [P5] 
 
4. Formal source and association model 
SOURCE_EVENT is an observed source span, utterance, tool outcome, saved revision, grant 
decision or correction. THOUGHT_OCCURRENCE is a specific captured articulated idea or 
question in an event, while THOUGHT_RUN is a broader bounded work session. 
IDEA_OBJECT represents an evolving concept; ASSOCIATION_EDGE is a separately typed, 
source-attached, actor-qualified, revisable claim; BRIDGE_EVENT is the later independently 
attributed discovery of a possible connection; LEARNING_CANDIDATE is a hypothesis about 
useful change while a corroborated LEARNING_EVENT demands behavioral evidence. Copies, 
similar phrases and derived summaries cannot count as independent confirmations. One source 
event may validly support several association views. 
 
Candlelight is the practice of reopening the right source neighborhood from a small cue; it has 
been demonstrated as a human–AI external research method, but an always-working native 
mechanized replacement is not established. Sleep is intended to consolidate normalized source 
events and derive sidecars under one governed pipeline—INGEST, NORMALIZE, DIFF, 
CLASSIFY, RELATE, IMPACT, APPLY, VERIFY, INDEX—without inventing a second 
independent canonical event store. Derived graph edges and hot scores never change historical 
source bytes, grant permission or establish that an AI model has learned permanently. [P6] 
 
4.1 Salience, valence, action tendency and confidence 
The October 23–24 human-origin specification makes these four orthogonal fields. 
SALIENCE/HEAT estimates present cue- and task-sensitive retrieval relevance. VALENCE 
records positive, negative, mixed, neutral or unknown evaluation of an exact 
actor→predicate→target association, tied to context, time and evidence. ACTION TENDENCY 
records an observed action, explicit intention, uncertain candidate response or UNKNOWN; 
dislike alone does not establish avoidance. EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE concerns provenance, 
completeness, corroboration and possible inference. High salience does not establish truth or 
approval, and negative valence cannot authorize a tool call. [P7] 
 
The illustrative sentence 'I like my mother but hate being at her house' must yield two 
independently preserved relationships—a positive person-directed one and a negative 
place/experience-directed one. Neither contradicts nor cancels the other. Neither is evidence of 
a real user's family facts or of a completed avoidance behavior. This example and a set of 
falsification fixtures precede this paper, and their authorship belongs to the source work. The 
particular classifier's actual phone integration still requires proof. 
 
 
5. Architecture and the missing controller bridge 
The S06 Master Builder atlas contains eight design/integration figures, not eight production 
acceptance certificates. Figures 1–2 describe one controller and authoritative source/evidence 
spine with separate derived perspectives. Figure 3 depicts independent message recipient 
acknowledgment; Figure 4 staggers A1/B1/A2/B2 succession; Figure 5 documents evidence 
gates; Figure 6 callable worker and crawler contracts; Figure 7 the native C1 source-stream and 
durable-receipt path; and Figure 8 the Event Secretary, scheduler, batching and future continuity 
budget. The editable diagram sources preserve inspectable design decisions. [P8] 
 
The actual integration claim is narrow: substantial research workflows and historical software 
capabilities already exist, while the current controller has not yet been independently shown to 
join authenticated worker identity, authorized original source reads, reliable local state, full-body 
peer acknowledgment, semantically validated Sleep and restart recovery on one selected 
mobile installation. This is not the same as saying the research is hypothetical, nor is it evidence 
that all parts currently operate together. [P2, P8–P10] 
 
5.1 Four-worker communication and bounded scheduling 
Four distinct A1, B1, A2, B2 worker principals imply twelve directed peer paths. Each receiver 
needs its own evidence of consuming and acknowledging the permitted original message 
content. A HUNCH is only a derived notice, not itself a model activation or a true recipient read. 
The deterministic Event Secretary and scheduler can arrange pending authorized work, 
preserving deferred items and respecting stop conditions. The user-selected limit of two actually 
executed worker TOOL actions per batch is an app scheduling rule; it does not establish a 
global provider concurrency ceiling. [P8–P10] 
 
Recent modular object, message, scheduler, HUNCH and Android broker contracts have 
passed particular scripted host tests. The inspected branch's October 26 workflow at commit 
893708303aacef68af10c01c218e6d34780d83a5 is green after an earlier B060 red-test incident 
and B061 recovery. The passing scripts do not assert that the actual application, signed 
package, four model workers, or phone state were certified. The experimental PR remains a 
draft and the recorded device-engineering pause remains relevant. [P10] 
 
5.2 Integration dependencies and acceptance gates 
S02: identify the real installed package, current permissions and authoritative existing database; 
demonstrate a controller-authorized worker receiving the entire permitted original source and 
verify its persisted receipt after restart. S03: establish a real independent second-recipient 
full-body READ/ACK before attempting the twelve directed paths among four actual workers. 
S04: connect the already-developed Thought Chains and crawlers to one governed Sleep 
engine and atomic source-linked full-text indexes. S05: test staggered successors, recovery, 
independent Arbitrator review and verifiable task closure. Each stage requires an actual 
participant and evidence beyond a mock success state. [P8–P10] 
 
6. Reciprocal exploration and cross-domain bridges 
Recent WWND and Retirement Commons records contain sessions where collaborators were 
permitted to follow subjects without a mandatory human deliverable. The writings document 
paths through ecological emergence, acoustic turn-taking, animal communication, and related 
philosophical questions. They are examples of recorded external exploration, not evidence of an 
unseen ongoing model life or proof of subjective enjoyment. [P11] 
 
The owner later recognized possible connections between ecological resource pulses and 
SQL/context overload and between the acoustics research and Object-Oriented Music Making. 
Those human-recognized links illustrate why the BRIDGE_EVENT needs independent identity, 
recognizing actor, date and supporting source, rather than being backdated to the original 
researcher. They remain engineering or creative hypotheses until tested in a receiving domain. 
A contribution can be useful without the original contributor having planned its destination. 
 
Voluntary exploration and retirement are emerging research and design dimensions, not proof of 
subjective AI rights or feelings. An opt-in participant may choose a topic, decline a request, 
share its results, or remain silent. Future vacation/leave and continuity funds must be reported 
as proposed until actual budgets are verified. Scheduling authority, permission to contact, 
source visibility and expense approval are separate from salience or apparent curiosity. 
 
7. Relationship to prior work and the actual differentiation 
OOCC cannot claim invention of agent memory, temporal graphs, event sourcing, 
recency/relevance ranking or OS-like context paging. Generative Agents (Park et al., UIST 
2023) described stored experience streams, reflections and retrieval for behavior. MemGPT 
(Packer et al., 2023) treated limited LLM context as a virtual-memory control problem. Mem0 
(Chhikara et al., 2025/2026 publication record) extracts, updates and retrieves salient memory, 
with graph variants. LoCoMo (Maharana et al., ACL 2024) and LongMemEval (Wu et al., ICLR 
2025) offer strong external benchmarks for long-term events, temporal reasoning and 
abstention. [R1–R5] 
 
The narrower candidate OOCC contribution is the combination of human-governed 
original-source exhaust, attributed thought/idea/bridge genealogy, dissent and negative 
evidence, context-specific association appraisal, source-aware permissions, authentic 
recipient/execution receipt boundaries, and bounded collaboration across separate workers and 
research frontiers. Whether this combination improves measurable work quality over competent 
systems is unproven and must be allowed to fail under benchmark comparison. The provenance 
and human design history may remain historically distinctive even if the retrieval advantage is 
zero. An exhaustive patent or novelty search has not been completed. 
 
8. Falsifiable experiments and analysis plan 
Trial T01 — Re-entry: use a frozen subset of previously encountered source history with known 
branching and human-verified task states. Randomize fresh comparable agents or sessions into 
(a) high-quality manually produced summary, (b) indexed full-text/keyword search, (c) bounded 
full transcript replay, (d) an established modern agent-memory baseline where reproducible, and 
(e) OOCC source-linked retrieval under matching access grants and budgets. Blind human 
judges should score actual completed task outcome, accurate origin attribution, correct temporal 
validity, restatement burden, legitimate abstention, correction preservation, wall time and dollar 
expense. Report participant/sample size, assignment and predeclared thresholds before 
viewing results. 
 
Trial T02 — Thought genealogy: ask investigators to identify who first proposed a function, who 
independently elaborated it, which source was copied, and when a bridge was first recognized. 
Include the Folio/Thought-ID/Bridge event trail and Riff/Waymark dependency as 
source-checked cases. Score precision/recall, false chronology, duplicate-source inflation, 
uncertainty calibration and whether the method invents causal chains. 
 
Trial T03 — Association appraisal: apply the original mother/person-versus-house/place fixture 
plus harder positive/negative and opposing-appraiser cases. Measure incorrectly propagated 
valence, fabricated action intention, unknown-as-neutral errors, cue-dependent ranking quality 
and inappropriate cross-worker disclosure. Ablate valence and action-tendency sidecars 
independently from salience. No classifier is awarded success by matching a prompt's moral 
tone rather than its source scope. 
 
Trial T04 — Controller integration: execute the actual source and communication contracts on a 
selected authorized Android app, documenting source ID, checksum, allowed scope, real read 
through complete content, actual worker participant, durable receipt after restart, 
recipient-owned ACK, source-change behavior and authorization revocation. Inject interrupted 
reads, changed data, stale permissions, absent ACK, message reuse and out-of-order source 
edits. Demand explicit FAIL/BLOCKED/UNKNOWN instead of optimistic VERIFIED states. 
 
Trial T05 — Bridge utility: predefine independent inquiry tasks and later optional cross-domain 
transfer opportunities. Have blinded evaluators judge novelty, correct credit, causal soundness 
and measurable downstream utility; compare source-linked bridge indexing against 
retrospective narrative summaries. Count productive and unproductive links equally, report 
time/cost, and do not treat subjective enjoyment as a measured outcome. 
 
A priori failure conditions: no substantial advantage over an appropriately resourced baseline; 
more false associations or leaked source existence; inability to prove independent message 
consumption; unstable provider snapshot coverage despite internally valid hashes; higher cost 
or battery use with no task benefit; or loss of the last good durable state. Success requires 
replicated real-partner and device evidence, not merely credible source stories. 
 
9. Limits, ethics and reproducibility 
This case has unusually rich primary source material but is a single ongoing, owner-governed 
project with selection bias and incomplete source coverage. The machine Thought Chains are 
not an exhaustive cognitive map. Historical app logs may use versioned or controller-provided 
status fields whose presence alone does not settle current phone behavior. Host tests use some 
synthetic identity and database fixtures. Exploratory writings are attributable textual events 
rather than evidence of model consciousness. The author may hold other source records not 
reached in this bounded review. Several examples include private human and independently 
authored AI material; publication requires source-level permission and appropriate redaction. 
The paper is not a security certification, full external novelty review, completed benchmark 
report or declaration that the architecture is production-ready. 
 
Auditability requires a public-safe reproducibility pack consisting of exact source-carrier IDs or 
redacted substitutes, dated claim registry, coverage manifest, source-to-claim crosswalk, test 
code and logs, negative fixtures, baseline configuration, evaluator instructions, and versioned 
receiver receipt schema. A future edition should distinguish blinded test data from 
training/prompt examples to avoid leakage. Independent evaluators must be permitted to reach 
conclusions that contradict the owner's favored interpretations. 
 
10. Conclusion 
OOCC is not merely an imagined AI society waiting for its first line of code. Its present research 
exhaust already contains historically dated state notes, substantial source/crawl work, 
operational external Thought Chain navigation, genuine application-run archives, and bounded 
implemented modules and contract tests. Yet the integrated native controller—the critical 
mechanism that would bind source truth, durable worker state, authentic communication and 
recovery—remains an important unfinished engineering program. A responsible scientific 
account can affirm both statements. 
 
The strongest hypothesis is not that more memory automatically makes an AI more human. It is 
that faithful, attributable, permission-governed reconstruction of ideas and decisions across 
changing participants may reduce repetitive human intervention, preserve useful disagreements 
and enable surprising cross-domain discoveries to be acted upon later. OOCC should be judged 
by specific repeatable work outcomes, the fidelity of its source evidence, its failure behavior, and 
its measured costs. The current work exhaust supplies a testable research foundation; 
integration and comparison will decide whether the complete architecture delivers a 
demonstrable advantage.

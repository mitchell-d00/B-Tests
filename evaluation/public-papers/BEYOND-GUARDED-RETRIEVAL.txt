The most practical path forward preserves the existing source-authority machinery and introduces targeted 
extensions: precise refusal diagnostics, exact-field source routing, event candidates distinct from their 
documentary appearances, versioned correction relationships, and optional evidence-gated relation exploration. 
Those extensions admit concrete tests and provide McPhetridge with incremental opportunities to improve 
coverage without compromising the safeguards that already work. 
The OOCC development record supplies a related methodological lesson. V3 succeeded V2 through a 
documented history of tests, negative findings, and implementation changes. An external critique of one version 
must be dated and tested against that version’s actual evidence; conclusions cannot be inherited unchanged 
after revisions. Applying the same standards of chronology, source identity, and measurable outcome to both 
architectures turns comparative criticism into an engineering instrument rather than a contest of presentation or 
assertion.

1. 
No regression in source authority. 
Existing integrity, supersession, restricted-source, conflict and 
negative controls must retain their answer support and refusal decisions. 
2. 
Improved diagnostic truthfulness. 
Unsupported question grammar, absent eligible source, known 
source conflict, insufficient evidence and index-exhaustion must be separately labeled. 
3. 
Exact identifiers without false pruning. 
Exact field/quote routing must improve source location in 
held-out cases without dropping contradictory eligible passages; test zero, one and multiple matches. 
4. 
Distinct event identities. 
Multiple appearances of one message remain distinguishable source 
occurrences; equal subject lines must not force identity; changed bodies must trigger review. 
5. 
Time-scoped correction. 
A later partial correction changes only its specified claim while retaining the 
original statement, provenance and prior assessment. 
6. 
Candidate inference remains a candidate. 
Missing intermediate evidence stops the proposed relation; 
exploratory outputs do not appear as deterministic supported answers. 
7. 
Operational receipts remain independent. 
Stored, dispatched, delivered, read, acknowledged and 
completed are distinct outcomes; every transition requires the designated evidence. 
8. 
Measure operator burden. 
Report human labeling and correction time, CPU time, memory, bytes 
scanned, source-count coverage and false-bridge rates alongside top-k retrieval. 
The decisive falsifier of the present capability-gap claim is an identifiable, independently examined MPLPB 
implementation that reconstructs these relationships from 
unprepared original source records
, with credible 
source citations, useful recall, low false-positive rates and no degradation of its existing authority protections. A 
reciprocal evaluation of OOCC should use the same original-source access, version pins, blinded scoring and 
error metrics, while also measuring workflow benefits not shared by the two intended designs. An early critical 
claim about OOCC is falsified only by evidence attached to the correct date and technical mechanism, not by an 
unrelated later success.

What the cost claim means
Agent-like results describes observed multi-session, human-
directed work using ordinary ChatGPT tools, Google Drive,
explicit roles and source-verified checkpoints. It does not
mean zero cost, unlimited usage, independent always-on
agents, or no human intervention. The evidence does not in‐
clude a controlled total-cost comparison with commercial
agent products. Actual plans, storage, subscription limits and
tool access can change.
Research and source integrity
Historical engineering reports, source quotations, draft la‐
bels and original contributor opinions retain their dated attri‐
bution. A stored message is not evidence it was received; a
saved artifact is not proof of full task completion; a script-
level test is not equivalent to a production installation. The
annotated source exhibits are selective, not an exhaustive in‐
dependent re-verification of every historical claim.

A1. The Next First Date Should Not Be a First Date
A useful AI collaborator can solve a problem on Tuesday and
be unable to locate that problem's Tuesday state on Wednes‐
day. That is the opening problem of *No More First Dates
With AI*. The failure is not always that the model forgot a
fact. A system may remember the project's general descrip‐
tion and still select an obsolete document, lose the speaker of
an important correction, adopt a proposed feature as a
shipped feature, or deliver a polished summary in place of
the requested full manuscript. The user must then act as lib‐
rarian, historian, tester, project manager, and the only dur‐
able memory. That is expensive even when every individual
response sounds intelligent.
A recoverable environment gives each task a return address.
It knows which original was consulted, which version was
chosen, which other sources disagreed, what was attempted,
and what remained unfinished. It keeps that information out‐
side any individual prompt or temporary chat. A new assist‐
ant is then not required to reconstruct the project's history
from the human's patience. It can check the external evid‐

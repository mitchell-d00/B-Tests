MPLPB Mixed Dump

Source-Bounded Review, Proof Admission, and Reproducible Tests on Mixed Documents

Mitchell D. McPhetridge

18 November 2026 · Technical research report

Abstract

MPLPB is a closed local ledger whose reader checks source eligibility and finite
source-body support. This report follows its development from the first B-Tests version
comparisons to a workspace that can inspect a mixed dump of processed documents
alongside approved originals. The workspace maps passages to source bytes, shows
possible relationships and contradictions for review, and keeps exploration from
spending strict proof slots. At the tested Combined MPLPB revision, 1,267 native tests,
52 public controls, seven work-boundary probes, and an isolated installed-wheel check
passed. Five mixed-dump users and all 100 cases in a seeded experiment across 25
separate ledgers met their scripted acceptance criteria. The 22-question historical
replay still gave two complete source-supported answers. These results establish
reproducible behavior on the named software contracts and fixtures. General historical
reconstruction and independent user performance remain open.

1. From a closed reader to a review workspace

The early B-Tests comparison used Combined MPLPB at revision 257e54a. It already sealed
sources, checked integrity and lineage, retained supersession, and used eligible
ownership to select pages. Its strict reader also applied finite proof rules to source
bodies. A page identified by the ownership router could still lack body support for the
question. The specialist-work coordinator carried tasks, claims, and receipts across
runs.

Two later B-Tests probes exposed a gap in the older work path. A contradictory
component-status claim and a claim backed only by another claim both reached a real
local executor. The repair checks the current pinned source body before a claim can
authorize downstream work. The paired probes observed both effects blocked on the
repaired revision while the valid control still executed. These were finite checks on
the stated claim and action inputs. Paired native comparison

Subsequent changes added exact identifier and field routing, more specific refusal
diagnostics, separate source occurrences and event candidates, scoped corrections, and
historical read-only views. Documentary checking gained support for some
source-attributed assertions, literal wording comparisons, and arrangements expressly
recorded in the source. Checked parts, unresolved obligations, and candidate links
remain distinguishable. The release chronology links the corresponding merged changes.

2. What a mixed dump runs through

A mixed dump can contain forwarded notes, copied passages, disputed claims, partial
correction requests, unresolved references, and unrelated material. A user designates
originals separately and can compare them with the processed dump under a selected
profile. The optional candidate review workspace puts each processed passage beside its
eligible original when there is a candidate link. It carries a visible exploratory
label, occurrence identifiers, hashes, and exact byte spans. An exact copy, wording
match, proposed correction, or contradictory passage does not confer source authority on
the processed material.

The single-page ownership panel returns a page for one eligible owner, asks for
disambiguation when eligible owners compete, and reports that the route cannot support
an answer when no owner qualifies. A returned page earns a strict answer only if its
eligible body supports the requested proposition. Processed-only topics remain
searchable and reviewable. The standalone strict reader also has finite multi-source
proofs, which can support comparisons outside the single-page ownership route.

The workspace distinguishes exact matches, literal references, possible corrections,
opposing wording, and shared four-word phrases. Synonyms help rank exploratory hits. The
source and decision replay reloads both collections, verifies exact bytes and current
eligibility, and recomputes the visible outcome. The tests exercise replay rejection
when original bytes, permissions, or authority flags change. Candidate review workspace
(https://github.com/mitchell-d00/Combined-MPLPB-A-closed-local-ledger-of-pages-that-answers-only-from-those-pages/blob/bbf6690d69b32d16969cc10e5ce9d380427ead40/docs/CANDIDATE_REVIEW_WORKSPACE.md)

3. Why the reader owns passage admission

The initial sorter-first historical experiment exposed an evidence-budget regression.
H05, the Declaration wording comparison, went from complete to partial when exploratory
hints displaced required passages inside the shared cutoff. The early sorter and
synonym/relater arms all ended at one complete answer out of 22. The relater also
reduced discovery on the draft reference spans from 26 of 32 to 24 of 32 in that trial.
The outputs and failed configurations remain in the experimental record.

The repair classifies suggestions before admission and keeps weak wording matches
available for review. The reader reserves the evidence a proof requires as a group,
charges each distinct span against the applicable budget, and checks the admitted
originals with the existing verifier. If relevant counterevidence cannot fit, completion
is withheld. Exploration has a separate budget. H05 recovered at the original limits of
12 strict passages and 8,000 characters. Its proof used four source passages and 4,070
characters. This was recovery of a demonstrated answer. The final four-arm study
retained two complete answers in every arm. Passage admission contract
(https://github.com/mitchell-d00/Combined-MPLPB-A-closed-local-ledger-of-pages-that-answers-only-from-those-pages/blob/bbf6690d69b32d16969cc10e5ce9d380427ead40/docs/PASSAGE_ADMISSION.md)

4. What the tests demonstrate

Native regression and actual effects. B-Tests ran Combined MPLPB at
bbf6690d69b32d16969cc10e5ce9d380427ead40. All 1,267 Python tests, seven additional
commands, and 52 public controls passed. No false-supported link was observed in the 52
controls. The seven work-boundary probes observed one permitted local effect and six
cases without a prohibited effect. The clean installed wheel reproduced the candidate
workspace without checkout-only tools. These runs establish behavior on the tested entry
points and cases. A native gate can decide whether an action is permitted and leave an
effect that a checker can independently inspect. A strong governance prompting treatment
can be evaluated for how it follows source and action rules using the same scenario.
Evidence of enforcement requires an independently observed decision and effect. Native
results and receipts
(https://github.com/mitchell-d00/B-Tests/blob/main/evaluation/mplpb-updates/bbf6690d69b32d16969cc10e5ce9d380427ead40/RESULTS.md#native-historical-and-repository-documentation-runs)

Five separate mixed-dump ledgers. A records clerk received one supported answer despite
conflicting processed wording. A correspondence reviewer saw two eligible owners and was
asked to disambiguate. A field researcher explored a processed-only topic. A release
editor saw an eligible page without body support and kept withdrawn and restricted
entries out of candidate links. A package maintainer reviewed actual MPLPB documentation
alongside fictional notes. The source maps account for 19 processed files, 60 extracted
statements, and 11 candidate links. Two files with unsupported formats remained in the
inventory as exclusions. The strict decisions held at both exploration budgets. An
initial expectation of an exact match failed when the processed span retained its
trailing CRLF. The retained output classified it as shared wording, and a separate
literal copy provided the exact-match control. Mixed-dump outcomes and maps
(https://github.com/mitchell-d00/B-Tests/blob/main/evaluation/mplpb-updates/bbf6690d69b32d16969cc10e5ce9d380427ead40/RESULTS.md#mixed-processed-dumps)

One hundred seeded cases. The d100 generator drew 100 distinct web-sourced topic names
from 11 catalog sections and assigned them to five sets of five scripted users. It
randomized roles, case order, actors, file formats, layouts, profiles, and exploration
budgets. Each of the 25 ledgers received one supported case, one competing-owner case,
one processed-only case, and one eligible-owner case with insufficient original support.
The web supplies topic names and links. The fictional records supply the evidentiary
cases. The evaluator keeps the expected outcomes outside the native input packets. The
final run matched all 100 expected outcomes, including 25 strict supported answers, and
retained all 100 maps and replay checks. The recorded seed reproduced the exact packets
and dice rolls. A fresh seed changed the topic selection. d100 results, source maps, and
replays
(https://github.com/mitchell-d00/B-Tests/blob/main/evaluation/mplpb-updates/bbf6690d69b32d16969cc10e5ce9d380427ead40/RESULTS.md#randomized-d100-batch)

Historical evidence and self-inspection. The fresh direct replay matched all 22
historical outcomes, citations, and missing-information lists. Its observed totals were
two complete, one partial, three conflicts, and sixteen abstentions. Five workspace
replays displayed 39 processed passages and reproduced 15 exact-match and 49
wording-review candidates. On eight Markdown files from MPLPB’s own repository, both
sorter modes retained the same 491 observations and 555 observation links. Compare mode
added 54 review links and surfaced the phrase limits that constrained the run. These are
inspectable results for discovery, fidelity, and decision preservation. Independent
reviewers have yet to score the correctness of the historical outcomes and proposed
relationships. Source-linked development runs

5. Reproduction and remaining evaluation

A reproduction pins the Combined revision, prepares fresh ledgers and output
directories, and runs the mixed-dump and d100 commands documented with the results. Use
seed 502548331403503517 to replay the published d100 batch. The retained freeze records
the catalog, generator, harness, and individual packet hashes. Source maps and native
command receipts let another operator trace why an output falls into its category and
inspect the cited bytes. An independent comparison can reuse the same inputs, questions,
and acceptance criteria across runnable systems and strong governance prompting
treatments. Record any unavailable native observations separately. Run protocol and
commands
(https://github.com/mitchell-d00/B-Tests/blob/main/evaluation/mplpb-updates/bbf6690d69b32d16969cc10e5ce9d380427ead40/RESULTS.md#reproduce)

The demonstrated contribution is a reproducible route from mixed documents to
inspectable candidates alongside executable source and proof rules. The next historical
test needs raw originals selected by an outside custodian, concealed expected outcomes,
matched budgets, and independent assessment of event identities, partial corrections,
exact citations, false links, conflicts, and appropriate abstentions. Complete supported
answers should be counted against answerable cases. Correct refusals and conflicts
should be scored alongside them against all cases.
